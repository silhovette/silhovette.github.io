About

Academic Background

I am an undergraduate student in Artificial Intelligence at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. My current work sits around large language model agents, planning and replanning, retrieval-augmented systems, and parameter-efficient adaptation.

I am interested in building AI systems that can use tools, coordinate across models or devices, and improve through structured feedback. My recent experience includes applied RAG systems, LLM fine-tuning, continual learning experiments, and agent-oriented research.

Telecom Customer Service Intent Recognition

Research Project

Built an intent recognition and target-plan prediction pipeline for multi-turn customer service dialogues, combining structured plan information, retrieval, few-shot reasoning, and LoRA/SFT experiments on open-source 7B models.

RAG Few-shot Reasoning LoRA/SFT Dialogue Understanding

LLM Continual Learning Research

In preparation

Explored parameter-efficient continual learning for language models, with an emphasis on replay-free LoRA merging, task-vector geometry, shared components, and task-specific residuals for balancing knowledge transfer and isolation.

Applied AI

Organized multi-source medical materials for multiple sclerosis and built a local retrieval-augmented QA prototype using ChromaDB and multilingual MiniLM embeddings, with source-aware evidence retrieval to reduce hallucination risk.
