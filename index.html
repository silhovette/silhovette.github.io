About

Academic Background

I am an undergraduate student in Artificial Intelligence at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, working on language-model systems with an emphasis on agents, planning and replanning, retrieval-augmented generation, and parameter-efficient adaptation.

My interests center on AI systems that connect reasoning with external tools, retrieval, and structured feedback. Recent work includes applied RAG prototypes, LLM fine-tuning, continual learning experiments, and early-stage research on training-aware agents.
