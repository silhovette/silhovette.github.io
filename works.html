Telecom Customer Service Intent Recognition

Applied AI Systems

Developed a dialogue-understanding pipeline for long, multi-turn telecom service sessions, covering intent recognition and target-plan prediction in a realistic service setting.

  • Cleaned, aggregated, and labeled 1,405 multi-turn customer service sessions.
  • Structured telecom plan information into retrievable candidate-plan knowledge.
  • Designed a RAG plus few-shot reasoning workflow over user plans, candidates, and dialogue context.
  • Ran LoRA/SFT experiments on open-source 7B models and compared them with baseline settings.

SnapKV KV Cache Compression Reproduction

Inference Systems

Reproduced the SnapKV training-free KV cache compression method and evaluated its latency, memory, and perplexity behavior in small-scale LLM inference settings.

  • Implemented post-prefill KV cache compression with observation-window scoring and pooling.
  • Compared baseline and compressed inference on WikiText and PG-19 workloads.
  • Recorded TTFT, TPOT, throughput, peak memory, FLOPs, and perplexity metrics.
  • Ran ablations over compression ratio, observation-window size, and pooling kernel size.
