Telecom Customer Service Intent Recognition

Industry Collab.

Built an intent recognition and target-plan prediction pipeline for long multi-turn customer service dialogues in a real telecom service setting.

  • Cleaned, aggregated, and labeled 1,405 multi-turn customer service sessions.
  • Structured telecom plan information and built retrieval support for candidate plans.
  • Designed a RAG plus few-shot reasoning workflow over user plans, candidates, and dialogue context.
  • Ran LoRA/SFT experiments on open-source 7B models and compared against baseline settings.

LLM Continual Learning Research

In progress

Explored parameter-efficient continual learning for language models, focusing on replay-free LoRA merging, task-vector geometry, and task-specific residual components.

  • Studied LoRA updates as task vectors in parameter space.
  • Worked with geometry-aware decomposition between shared and private task components.
  • Built continual learning experiment workflows over multiple text classification sequences.
  • Supported training logs, metric aggregation, and ablation analysis.

AI4MS Medical Knowledge Graph & RAG System

Applied AI

Built a local retrieval-augmented QA prototype for multiple sclerosis knowledge organization and evidence-grounded medical question answering.

  • Parsed and organized multi-source medical materials into searchable knowledge assets.
  • Used ChromaDB and multilingual MiniLM embeddings to index medical evidence chunks.
  • Mapped user questions to relevant evidence passages with source-aware retrieval.
  • Focused on reducing hallucination risk by preserving citations in generated answers.
