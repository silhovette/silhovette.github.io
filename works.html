Telecom Customer Service Intent Recognition

Applied AI Systems

Built an intent recognition and target-plan prediction pipeline for long multi-turn customer service dialogues in a real telecom service setting.

SnapKV KV Cache Compression Reproduction

Inference Systems

Reproduced the SnapKV training-free KV cache compression method and evaluated its latency, memory, and perplexity behavior on small-scale LLM inference.

  • Implemented post-prefill KV cache compression with observation-window scoring and pooling.
  • Compared baseline and compressed inference on WikiText and PG-19 workloads.
  • Recorded TTFT, TPOT, throughput, peak memory, FLOPs, and perplexity metrics.
  • Ran ablations over compression ratio, observation window size, and kernel size.
